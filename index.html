About Katlyn

Katlyn is a junior distance runner who approaches cross country and track with consistency, coachability, and a team-first mindset. She sets ambitious goals, embraces the daily work behind improvement, and continues to build confidence and resilience through competition.

Her selection as the first SKG Distance athlete named Gwinnett County Runner of the Week reflects the commitment she brings to training and race day. As she looks ahead to college, Katlyn is focused on continuing to grow as both a student and an athlete and contributing to a program that values hard work, learning, and team success.
